Fime Privacy Policy

Last updated: 26 November 2026

Fime is a personal finance app for recording income, expenses, budgets, savings goals, subscriptions and loans. This policy explains what information the app handles, where it goes and the choices you have. Questions can be sent to shauritangaathanas@gmail.com.

1. Information kept on your phone

Fime works mainly on your device. These are stored on your phone and are not sent to us:

  • Your profile: name, phone number and, if you choose one, a profile photo. Fime only uses a photo you pick.
  • Your password, stored only as a salted hash, not as the password itself.
  • Your financial records: transactions, categories, budgets, savings goals, subscriptions, recurring entries and loans, including lenders' names and notes.
  • Your reminder and app settings.

The account you create exists only on your phone. We cannot see it, recover it or reset its password. Depending on your phone's backup settings, Android may include app data in the backup kept in your Google account. That backup is handled by Google under your settings.

2. The money assistant (optional)

The assistant is off until you agree to turn it on. It needs an internet connection. When you use it, the following is sent over an encrypted connection to the Fime server and then to our AI service provider so that your question can be answered:

  • Your question and a few earlier questions and answers from the same chat.
  • Today's date and the records needed to answer, such as amounts, dates, categories, transaction titles, budgets, savings goal names, subscription and recurring entry names, and loan titles, amounts and due dates.
  • If you use the microphone, your voice recording, so that it can be turned into text. Recording happens only while you record a question.

The app does not send your password, your profile name, phone number or photo, lenders' names or loan notes. Text you type yourself, such as a transaction title or a question, is sent as written, so avoid putting sensitive personal details in it if you do not want them sent.

If you ask it to, the assistant can create or change a budget on your phone. It cannot make payments or move money. Its answers can be wrong; check important figures.

You can stop using the assistant at any time by opening its menu and choosing Turn off assistant. The rest of Fime keeps working.

3. Our server and service providers

  • Fime server. It passes your request to the AI service and returns the reply. It does not save your questions, answers, records or recordings. It uses your IP address briefly to limit misuse, and keeps technical logs such as the time, size and success of requests. Standard server and security logs may include IP addresses.
  • Groq, Inc. provides the AI models that write answers and turn speech into text. Information sent to Groq is processed under Groq's privacy policy, and may be processed outside your country.

We do not sell your information, show advertising or use advertising or behaviour-tracking tools. We may disclose information if the law requires it.

4. Permissions

  • Microphone: only for voice questions to the assistant.
  • Notifications: reminders you turn on.
  • Photos: only to set a profile picture you choose.
  • Internet: for the assistant.

5. Keeping and deleting information

Information on your phone stays until you delete it. To delete your account and all your records, open Fime, tap your profile picture on the Home screen and choose Delete account and data. Uninstalling Fime, or clearing its storage in your phone's settings, also removes the app's data from your phone; remove any Android backup through your Google account settings. Logging out does not delete your data. The chat is kept only while the app is open and is cleared when you start a new chat, log out or close the app. The Fime server does not keep a copy of your records to delete. For a question or a deletion request, email us.

If you export your transactions, you choose where the file goes, and the app or service you send it to handles it under its own terms.

6. Security

Data sent to the Fime server travels over HTTPS. Passwords are hashed. No method of storage or transmission is completely secure, so protect your phone with a screen lock.

7. Children

Fime is intended for adults and is not directed at anyone under 18.

8. Changes

We will update this page when the policy changes and revise the date above.
